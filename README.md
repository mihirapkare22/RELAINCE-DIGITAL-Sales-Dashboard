# RELAINCE-DIGITAL-Sales-Dashboard

 Objective: Developed an interactive Excel dashboard to analyze Reliance Digital sales performance, 
identify product and sales trends, and support data-driven business decisions. 

 Cleaned and analyzed sales data using Excel formulas, Pivot Tables, and Pivot Charts to track 
KPIs such as Total Sales, Revenue, Quantity Sold, Average Discount, and Product Performance. 

 Implemented interactive slicers and visualizations to analyze sales by product, referral source, 
payment method, and year, enabling quick identification of key business insights.
